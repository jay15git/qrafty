Note on react-doctor: `react-doctor` is a standalone CLI (`pnpm doctor`),
not an ESLint plugin. Suppress its findings with the native directive
`// react-doctor-disable-next-line react-doctor/<rule> -- <reason>`.
The `// eslint-disable-next-line react-doctor/<rule>` form makes ESLint fail
with "Definition for rule not found" — do not use it.
